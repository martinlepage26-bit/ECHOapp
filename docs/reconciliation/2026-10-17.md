## Scope and limitations

- This reconciles the current reader into GitHub. It does not merge or deploy automatically.
- Base44 account data, paid voices and unfinished cloud cloning were not migrated.
- Browser online voices may send text to the browser voice provider. Only voices marked on device report localService=true.
- Worker guard tests cover route and literal-address behavior, not a complete DNS or security audit.
- The stale cached search result showed an older dictation console; direct live inspection supersedes it.
