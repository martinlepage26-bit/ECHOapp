# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Writers, editors, students, and reviewers use ECHO when they need to hear text back in a natural voice, catch awkward phrasing, and move between reading and dictation without leaving the same surface. The primary workflow is practical and task-driven: paste or import text, listen closely, and revise from what the ear catches faster than the eye.

## Product Purpose
ECHO turns drafted language into a dependable readback workflow on the same device. It exists to make spoken review feel fluid rather than robotic, to keep word tracking aligned with the voice, and to let users move between imported documents, pasted drafts, and dictated notes without browser-specific surprises.

## Brand Personality
Calm, technical, focused. The interface should feel quiet and competent: a restrained console-like tool that keeps attention on the words and the voice instead of on ornamental UI.

## Anti-references
Do not let ECHO feel like a generic AI chat pane, a rounded consumer note app, or a loud media player. Avoid robotic fallback voices, decorative chrome, autoplay fragility framed as user error, and markdown-literal readback that speaks symbols instead of meaning.

## Design Principles
1. Put the voice first: readback quality and continuity outrank decorative UI behavior.
2. Keep the interface legible but recessive so the draft and the active word do the visual work.
3. Normalize rough source text into natural speech without making the user manually clean formatting first.
4. Make browser behavior converge through explicit playback handling, not assumptions about autoplay or platform defaults.
5. Preserve a tight loop between listening, spotting issues, and revising.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target reliable cross-browser behavior, readable contrast, and clear fallback messaging when playback or permissions fail. Respect reduced-motion expectations by keeping motion minimal and state-driven. Readback highlighting must stay in sync with spoken pace so users who rely on audio plus visual tracking are not forced to choose one mode over the other.
