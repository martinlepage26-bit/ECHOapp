# ECHO product

A compact reader for hearing and following text, with useful pronunciation of numbers in English and French.

Current scope: browser speech, sentence tracking and seeking, adjustable speed, language-aware voice selection, local document import, public URL extraction and local drafts. Files and drafts remain browser-local; online browser voices and URL import have network data flows described in the README.

The Base44 app has separate provider-managed cloud voices and account-backed drafts. They remain a documented difference, not an implied feature of this repository. Voice cloning and cross-device draft synchronization are not delivered by this reconciliation. This is a statement about the present release, not a permanent exclusion from the product.
